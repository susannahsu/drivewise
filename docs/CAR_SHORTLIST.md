# DriveWise — Seed Car Shortlist (Top 20 Best-Value, 1-Car Household)

_Last updated: 2026-06-23 · This is the MVP seed catalog for US-3 (model picker)._

## How this list was built

Optimized for **total cost of ownership** — the things that actually drain money
over 5+ years: **depreciation** (the #1 cost), **reliability/repair cost**,
**fuel economy**, **insurance**, and **resale strength**. Sourced from 2026
lowest-cost-of-ownership rankings (CarEdge, U.S. News Best Cars for the Money)
cross-checked against reliability reputation.

**Key constraint from your inputs: no home charging.** That deliberately
**deprioritizes pure EVs** — without a home charger, public/DC-fast charging
erases most of the EV running-cost advantage and adds time cost. So this list
leans **efficient gas + hybrid**, which is the genuine value sweet spot for your
situation. (If you ever get home/workplace charging, we'd add the Tesla Model 3,
Hyundai Ioniq 6, and Chevy Equinox EV — flagged at the bottom.)

Prices are approximate 2026 starting MSRP; treat as ballpark.

## If your situation changes

- **Get home/workplace charging?** Add **Tesla Model 3**, **Hyundai Ioniq 6**,
  **Chevy Equinox EV** — they'd jump up the value ranking with cheap home
  charging.
- **Need 3 rows / lots of cargo?** Add **Kia Sorento Hybrid** and **Toyota
  Highlander Hybrid** (value end of 3-row).
- **Minimal miles / city only?** The own-vs-Uber analysis (US-2) may beat all of
  these — don't skip it.
